The problem

Most corporate training doesn't work

You already know this. The completion rates look fine, but nothing actually changes on the floor.

Slide decks get clicked through

Learners rush to the quiz, guess until they pass, and forget everything by Monday. The training box is checked but the skill gap stays open.

Live role-play is expensive to scale

Bringing a facilitator into a room with ten employees costs time and money every single session. You can't run it across locations or on demand.

Generic modules teach generic skills. Your customer complaints, safety protocols, and compliance cases are specific to your business.

How it works

From your scenario to a running simulation

Three steps. You describe the training scenario, I build it, and you drop the finished package into your LMS.

1

Share your scenario

Describe the situation your employees need to practice. A difficult customer, a safety incident, a compliance conversation. I'll ask the right questions to turn it into a simulation brief.

2

I build the simulation

A 3D environment with an AI character that holds a real conversation, reacts to what the learner says, shows emotion, and scores their performance on the criteria you care about.

3

Drop it in your LMS

You get a SCORM 1.2 or 2004 package. Upload it to your LMS the same way you upload any other course. Scores, completion, and session data report back automatically.
